01 — Explore the app

Testing without hardware

You can explore DriftLed even when a controller is not nearby.

  1. Open Settings in the DriftLed app.
  2. Scroll to the System section.
  3. Enable Demo mode.
02 — Troubleshooting

Connection checklist

If DriftLed cannot find or connect to your controller, try these quick checks first:

  • Make sure the DriftLed controller is powered on and nearby.
  • Confirm that Bluetooth access is enabled for DriftLed.
  • For Wi-Fi connections, allow Local Network access on Apple devices.
  • Check that your device is connected to the controller’s local Wi-Fi network.
  • Close and reopen the app, then try connecting again.

If the problem continues, email us and include your device model, operating-system version, connection method, and a short description of what you see.
